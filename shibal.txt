PARENT / GUARDIAN CONSENT FORM
MABIYASA: 2.5D Kapampangan Educational RPG (At-Home Playtest)
Department of Computer Studies – Pampanga State University (Sto. Tomas Campus)

1. Purpose of the Activity
Your child/ward is invited to participate in a remote Alpha Playtesting session for MABIYASA, an educational 2.5D pixel role-playing game (RPG) developed as a Capstone Research Project. The objective of this playtest is to evaluate the game’s usability, sentence-building card mechanics, and educational effectiveness in teaching the Kapampangan language.

2. Activity Details & Home Requirements
Activity: At-home gameplay testing session and completion of an online evaluation feedback form.

Location: At the student's home / personal device.

Requirements: A Windows PC / compatible device with internet connection to download the game installer/executable and access the online feedback questionnaire.

Estimated Duration: Approximately [Insert Time, e.g., 30–45 minutes] of playtime and questionnaire completion.

3. Voluntary Participation & Safety
Participation in this activity is completely voluntary and done during the student's free time at home.

Your child may stop playing or discontinue the evaluation at any time without any effect on their academic standing.

The game executable file provided is verified, safe, free of malware, and designed solely for research evaluation.

4. Confidentiality & Data Privacy
All responses collected via the evaluation form will be kept strictly confidential and used exclusively for academic research and capstone project documentation.

No sensitive personal data or private device information will be accessed or collected.
